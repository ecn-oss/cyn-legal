Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyn, a Discord bot developed and maintained by COPPER-9, processes and stores information when used on Discord servers.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, Cyn may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Ticket subjects and descriptions submitted by users.
  • Moderation reasons provided by server staff.
  • Server membership and role information.
  • Welcome images and image URLs configured by administrators.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to provide Cyn's features, including:

  • Creating and managing support tickets.
  • Configuring welcome messages and images.
  • Managing server roles and permissions.
  • Providing moderation commands.
  • Displaying user and server information.
  • Maintaining the bot's functionality.

3. Data Storage

Cyn stores certain server configuration data on its hosting environment.

This may include ticket settings, welcome settings, channel IDs, role IDs, server IDs and references to configured images.

Uploaded welcome images may also be stored locally in the bot's hosting environment.

4. Support Tickets

When a user creates a support ticket, the subject and description they submit are displayed in a private Discord channel.

The ticket channel is accessible to the ticket creator, authorized support roles and the bot according to the configured Discord permissions.

When a ticket is closed, Cyn attempts to delete its Discord channel. This does not guarantee deletion of copies, screenshots or information retained independently by Discord or other users.

5. Data Sharing

COPPER-9 does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and the hosting provider as necessary for Cyn to operate.

6. Data Retention

Configuration information may remain stored while Cyn is being used on a server.

Information displayed in Discord channels may remain subject to Discord's own data retention practices and the actions of server administrators.

7. User Choices

Users can choose what information they submit through support tickets.

Server administrators can modify or remove Cyn's configurations and uninstall the bot from their servers.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are intended to protect the information handled by Cyn. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Cyn's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact COPPER-9 through its official support channels.

11. Discord Disclaimer

Cyn is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc.
